The AI trends panel answers two questions without leaving the app: is there a newer `claude` or `codex` than the one
I have, and what changed in it? And what's new in AI tooling that a developer could actually use?

<Shot id="trends" caption="My CLIs: your installed version against the latest releases." />

## Open the panel

Click **AI trends** (the newspaper icon) at the bottom of the sidebar. A dot on the icon means a CLI version newer
than yours is out.

The panel has two views: **My CLIs** and **Tech**. Use **Search titles** to filter either list.

## My CLIs

For Claude Code and Codex, the panel shows your installed version (**Installed …**, or **Not installed**) next to
recent releases:

- If you're behind, it lists the newer releases with a few lines on what each one **added**.
- If you're up to date, it shows what your version added.

**Open release notes** opens the full notes in your browser.

## Tech news

**Tech** collects releases, libraries, APIs, techniques, papers with code and notable new repositories. Switch
**Region** between:

- **Global** — sources such as the OpenAI News, Cursor and GitHub changelogs, the Hugging Face blog and daily papers,
  new GitHub repos, Hacker News, Simon Willison and Latent Space.
- **Korea** — GeekNews, Naver D2, and the Kakao, Toss, Woowahan, Daangn and LY tech blogs (AI posts only).

Items are grouped by topic — **Subagents**, **Hooks**, **Agent view**, **ADE**, **Harness**, **MCP & skills**,
**Context engineering**, **Vibe & agentic coding** — and everything else goes under **Latest**. A dot marks items
that are new since your last visit. Select an item to see a short excerpt, then **Open** it (or **HN discussion**
for Hacker News items).

<Aside type="note" title="Filtered for developers">
  Business news — stock prices, funding, earnings, executive moves, lawsuits, policy — is filtered out of every
  source. The filter is a fixed set of rules, not an AI model, so it's predictable; it does mean few items land in
  the topic groups.
</Aside>

## When it fetches

Nothing is fetched in the background. The panel fetches when you open it, if its cache is older than 30 minutes;
↻ **Refresh** fetches right away. A source that fails keeps its last items and is named at the bottom of the panel.
Items you've already seen drop off after a week.

## Claude summary

Select **✦ Claude summary** and click **Summarize with Claude** for a few lines on the recent news.

<Aside type="caution" title="Uses your Claude quota">
  The summary runs only when you click. It sends titles and short excerpts only, and uses the current project's
  Claude account (Haiku model).
</Aside>

## Apply a trend to a project

On a release or news item, **Apply to project** asks Claude to analyse what that change would mean for the current
project and shows it as a graph. The analysis changes no files and uses your Claude quota (Sonnet). If you then
apply it, only the steps you tick are pasted into a session in a new [worktree](/en/docs/agents/worktrees/).
Local projects only.
