Split the window to watch several sessions at once: Claude writing code next to Codex reviewing it, or an agent next
to the shell running your tests. You can have up to **four panes**, and each one shows one session.

<Shot id="split" caption="Two panes side by side. The focused pane's session is the active tab." />

## Split a pane

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘D</kbd> | Split right |
| <kbd>⇧⌘D</kbd> | Split down |

The focused pane splits in two and the new, empty pane gets focus. Open a session in it with <kbd>⌘T</kbd>,
<kbd>⇧⌘T</kbd> or <kbd>⌥⌘T</kbd>, or click one of the buttons in the empty pane. With four panes, splitting does
nothing.

<kbd>⌘K</kbd> → **Split right** / **Split down** work too.

### Split by dragging a tab

Drag a tab from the tab bar onto the terminal area:

- drop it on a pane's **edge** (left, right, top or bottom) to split that pane there, or
- drop it in the **middle** of a pane to show that session in it instead.

If the session was already showing in another pane, it moves, and its old pane closes.

## Mix projects

The split layout belongs to the whole window, not to one project, so panes can show sessions from different projects.
When you focus a pane from another project, the sidebar and the tab bar switch to that project.

Selecting a project in the sidebar focuses a pane that already shows it; otherwise the focused pane shows that
project's last session.

## Move between panes

Click a pane, or press <kbd>⌥⌘</kbd> plus an arrow key:

| Keys | Moves focus |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌥⌘←</kbd> | Left |
| <kbd>⌥⌘→</kbd> | Right |
| <kbd>⌥⌘↑</kbd> | Up |
| <kbd>⌥⌘↓</kbd> | Down |

The focused pane's session is the active tab, and that's where your typing goes.

Each pane has a head with the session's status dot, its title, the project (and the branch for a worktree), and
buttons to zoom and close it.

## Zoom one pane

Press <kbd>⇧⌘↩</kbd>, double-click the pane's head, or click **⤢** in it. The pane fills the stage while the others
wait in place. Press it again (or click **⤡**) to go back. Opening a new split also ends the zoom.

### Full window

Press <kbd>⇧⌘F</kbd> to hide the sidebar and tabs too and show only the focused pane. Press <kbd>⇧⌘F</kbd> again to
leave.

<Aside type="note">
  <kbd>Esc</kbd> doesn't leave zoom or full window. It belongs to the CLI, which uses it to interrupt the agent. Use the
  same shortcut you entered with.
</Aside>

## Resize panes

- Drag the divider between two panes.
- Or press <kbd>⌥⇧⌘</kbd> plus an arrow key to move the focused pane's divider.
- **Double-click a divider** to split the space evenly again.

## Close a pane

Click **×** in the pane's head, or press <kbd>⌘W</kbd> in an empty pane. Closing a pane only removes it from the
layout: the session it showed keeps running as a tab. To end the session itself, close its tab with <kbd>⌘W</kbd>.

## All split shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘D</kbd> / <kbd>⇧⌘D</kbd> | Split right / down (up to 4 panes) |
| <kbd>⌥⌘←</kbd> <kbd>⌥⌘→</kbd> <kbd>⌥⌘↑</kbd> <kbd>⌥⌘↓</kbd> | Move focus between panes |
| <kbd>⇧⌘↩</kbd> | Zoom pane / restore |
| <kbd>⇧⌘F</kbd> | Full window / restore |
| <kbd>⌥⇧⌘←</kbd> <kbd>⌥⇧⌘→</kbd> <kbd>⌥⇧⌘↑</kbd> <kbd>⌥⇧⌘↓</kbd> | Resize the pane |
| <kbd>⌘W</kbd> | Close session (in an empty pane: close the pane) |

The layout is saved and comes back when you relaunch; see [Restore and power](/en/docs/workspace/restore-and-power/).
