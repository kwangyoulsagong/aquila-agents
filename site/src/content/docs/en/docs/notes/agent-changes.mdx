Agents edit your notes too — they tick off checklists, update a spec's status, rewrite a section. Aquila remembers
what each note looked like when you last saw it, so you can see exactly what changed while you were away.

<Shot id="noteReview" caption="The change bar on top of a note, with the diff open." />

## The change bar

When you open a note that changed since you last saw it, a bar appears above the text:

- **Claude changed this note · +12 −3** — a Claude session wrote to it.
- **Changed outside · +12 −3** — something else did: another editor, a script, `git pull`.

The numbers are the lines added and removed since your last look.

## Review the changes

<Steps>

1. Click **Review** in the bar. The diff between what you last saw and the file now opens below it.

2. For a part you don't want, click **Revert hunk**, then confirm **Revert this part?**. Only that part goes back to
   what you saw; the rest of the agent's edit stays.

3. When you're happy with the note, click **Mark as seen**. The bar goes away until the next outside change.

</Steps>

A revert is a normal edit in the editor, saved like your own typing.

## Notes that changed elsewhere

The editor's side column has a **Changed outside** list: other notes that changed since you last looked, with who
changed them (**Claude** or **outside**) and when. Click one to open it and review. The list is hidden when there's
nothing to show.

## What counts as "seen"

Aquila records a note as seen when:

- you open it in the editor and nothing outside has changed it since your last look,
- you save your own edits on top of the version you saw, or
- you click **Mark as seen**.

A write from an agent, a terminal or git never counts as seen — that difference is what the bar shows.

<Aside type="note">
  Aquila decides "Claude" from what its Claude sessions report. It can occasionally name an earlier session than the
  one that made the edit.
</Aside>

## Where this is stored

The copies of what you saw are kept only in `~/.aquila/notes-seen/` on your Mac — never next to the note and never in
your repo. Up to 2,000 notes (50 MB) are kept; the least recently used are dropped first. Notes larger than 2 MB aren't
tracked.

Nothing is polled in the background: the check happens when you open a note.
